Acknowledgement of Country

Statement

Meowdia was created by students of Swinburne Vietnam in Can Tho, a city in the Mekong Delta. This region is shared by many communities, including the Kinh majority and the Khmer, Hoa and other ethnic groups whose cultures have shaped it over generations. We acknowledge these communities and pay our respects to their traditions and elders, post and present. We recognise that language, culture and connection to place are central to who people are.

We are also building this site for an audience in Australia, and we acknowledge Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples as the Traditional Owners and Custodians of Country throughout Australia.

Why this is here

Creative work shapes how people see themselves and each other, so we start from respect. Recognising the peoples who share Can Tho is the first step in showing the workplace Meowdia wants to be: one that welcomes people of every ethnic background, language and culture. We want our work to represent the communities around us fairly and with care.

What we do about it

We encourage applications from people of all ethnic backgrounds, including Khmer, Hoa and other ethnic minority communities.

We design this site to be accessible, so everyone can use it.

We do not use cultural symbols, patterns, music or festival imagery from any community without permission and credit.

We commit to learning from the communities around us and to listening when they tell us how we can represent them better.
